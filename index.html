The buttons

  • 1 person: a hand at each end of the lanes. Tap for 2 people.
  • 2 people: a person at each end, two hands each.
  • Zips: patterns with handoffs from one hand to the other. With 2 people, z is a pass to the other person and s a pass to your own other hand.
  • Reverses: patterns in which lanes change direction. Works with 1 or 2 people.
  • Mixed: two people, each keeping their own rhythm of single rolls and pairs.
  • Slope: toss-juggling patterns. You sit at the bottom, place balls up the sloping lanes, and they roll back to you.
  • Random top 5: plays one of the five starred patterns.
  • Random all modes: plays a starred pattern from any mode, and switches to that mode.
  • Red X: shows while a mode is on. Tap it to go back to plain 1 person or 2 people.

A green button is switched on. A faded button cannot be used until the green one is switched off.
