The buttons

A green button is switched on. A faded button cannot be used until the green one is switched off.

  • On the board
  • The lanes: tap to pause or play. Drag sideways to move the pattern forward or back.
  • 1 person: a hand at each end of the lanes. Tap for 2 people.
  • 2 people: a person at each end, two hands each.
  • Zips: patterns with handoffs from one hand to the other. A z is always a zip to your own other hand. With 2 people, a p is a pass to the other person.
  • Reverses: patterns in which lanes change direction. Works with 1 or 2 people.
  • Mixed: two people, each keeping their own rhythm of single rolls and pairs.
  • Slope: toss-juggling patterns. You sit at the bottom, place balls up the sloping lanes, and they roll back to you.
  • Ping Pong: quick patterns where each hand places a ball a little way up a lane, then grabs the one that just rolled past, so lanes are used both ways. Works with 1 or 2 people.
  • Random top 5: plays one of the five starred patterns.
  • Random all modes: plays a starred pattern from any mode, and switches to that mode.
  • Red X: shows while a mode is on. Tap it to go back to plain 1 person or 2 people.
  • Along the bottom
  • All patterns: every pattern for the mode you are in, on one page. Tap one to play it.
  • Balls − and +: one ball fewer or more. Some patterns only work with certain numbers, so it skips the ones that don't.
  • Right / Left: which hand (or person) rolls first, in lane 1. Swaps the pattern for its mirror image.
  • Speed: slide to slow the balls down or speed them up.
  • Pause / Play: stops and starts the balls.
  • Beat sound: tap to go from off, to a click on every roll (higher for the right hand, lower for the left), to the numbers said out loud, in one voice for the right hand (or person) and another for the left. Numbers rolled at the same moment are said together as one quick phrase.
  • Favourites: save the pattern you are watching, or open the ones you saved. Add patterns to transitions to play them one after another, joined smoothly, each as many times as you like.
  • Share: copy or share a link that opens this exact pattern, or download a video of it that loops.
  • (i): shows what the numbers and colours mean.
  • Arrow: on a phone on its side: hides the controls to give the lanes more room. Tap again to bring them back.
  • Fullscreen: hides the browser's bars, like a video. Tap again to leave.

Transitions

The patterns play one after another, each as many times as its number, joined by the rolls that get the balls from one into the next. They all need the same number of balls. In a zip run, h is a ball held for a beat. Saved in this browser.
